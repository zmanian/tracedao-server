# Trace Commons v0.12.2

Sign in to NEAR AI with GitHub, Google or a NEAR wallet from any desktop app. Wallet sign-in asks you to sign a login message; it does not request a transaction or spending key.

- Open NEAR AI Cloud billing for the organization associated with your inference key. The app checks that the account connection still matches before opening the browser.
- On macOS, open Private AI before joining a commons. Capture choices remain explicit, and signing in does not enroll you or enable contributions.
- Clearer tool cards show connection status and keep configuration details available when you need them.
- Updated account copy explains what signing in stores and what access it grants.

Billing opens the NEAR AI Cloud website; payments and spending limits are managed there.
